1. Introduction & Scope (परिचय)

Welcome to Shri Ramji Astro ("SRA", "we", "us", or "our"), available at https://shriramjiastro.in and via our Patrika progressive web application. SRA provides high-precision Vedic astrological calculation software, digitized Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS) algorithmic engines, Muhurta calculators, bespoke Parashara horoscope reports, and educational research articles.

We recognize the deeply personal and sacred nature of astrological birth records (Janma Kundli). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, processing, storage, and safeguarding of your information when you visit our website, use our calculation tools, purchase reports, or communicate with us.

Legal Compliance: This policy is formulated in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 of India.

2. Information We Collect (एकत्रित जानकारी)

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to perform mathematical planetary calculations and deliver requested services:

A. Astrological & Birth Data (ज्योतिषीय जन्म विवरण)

  • Date of Birth & Exact Time of Birth: Required to compute planetary longitudes, Ascendant (Lagna), Nakshatra degrees, and Vimshottari dasha periods.
  • Place of Birth (City / Latitude & Longitude): Required to calculate local sidereal time, geocentric coordinates, and topocentric house cusps using NASA JPL DE431 Swiss Ephemeris standards.
  • Gender & Name: Used strictly for labeling charts and customized horoscope report generation.

B. Account & Authentication Data

  • Phone Number & Email Address: Used for secure OTP-based login (via Firebase / SMS authentication), order verification, and dispatching digital horoscope reports (PDFs) to your inbox.
  • Saved Kundli Records: If logged in, birth profiles you choose to save in your private account dashboard for future reference.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry

  • Device & Network Identifiers: IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and timestamp logs for server security, rate limiting, and DDoS prevention.
  • Application Preferences: Theme selection (day/night mode) and language preference (Hindi/English) stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Data (डेटा का उपयोग)

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate, declared astrological and technical purposes:

  • Astronomical & Vedic Computation: Executing real-time ephemeris algorithms to render D1 Lagna, D9 Navamsha, D60 Shashtiamsha, Ashtakavarga, Shadbala, and planetary transits (Gochar).
  • Report Generation & Fulfillment: Compiling personalized bespoke astrological analyses (such as the Mega Parashara Report) and delivering them electronically.
  • Account Continuity: Allowing you to access your saved charts and purchase history across devices.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries regarding calculation interpretations, order delivery, or billing questions.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Guarding against unauthorized automated scraping of our ephemeris engines, brute-force attacks, and payment fraud.

4. Payments & Financial Information (भुगतान सुरक्षा)

When purchasing premium reports, consultations, or software access, your financial transaction is processed directly through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay).

Important: Shri Ramji Astro never collects, processes, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. All sensitive payment tokens are handled entirely within the encrypted sandbox of the certified payment gateway.

We receive only transaction metadata necessary for accounting and service fulfillment, including the Gateway Payment ID, Order ID, amount paid, timestamp, and transaction status (Success/Failure).

5. Non-Disclosure & Data Sharing (डेटा साझाकरण निषेध)

We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or birth data. Your chart information will never be shared with data brokers, ad networks, or commercial telemarketers.

Information is shared only under the following strict operational necessities:

  • Infrastructure & Service Providers: Trusted cloud hosting providers (e.g., Render, Firebase, Supabase) and transactional communication gateways (e.g., transactional email/SMS dispatchers) operating under strict data confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If mandated by law, court order, or governmental authority possessing lawful jurisdiction to investigate fraud or security breaches.

6. Storage, Cookies & Progressive Web App (कुकीज़ व स्टोरेज)

Shri Ramji Astro is engineered as a high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA). We minimize server dependencies by utilizing client-side technologies:

  • Local Storage (HTML5 localStorage): Stores non-sensitive preferences such as your chosen UI theme (Day/Night), selected language (Hindi/English), and recent locally-generated charts so you can access them offline without network latency.
  • Service Workers (Cache Storage): Caches static assets (stylesheets, fonts, mathematical scripts) to provide instant load speeds and offline capability.
  • No Invasive Advertising Trackers: We do not load third-party ad retargeting pixels, behavioral surveillance scripts, or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention & Erasure Rights (डेटा अधिकार)

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), you hold complete ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Guest Calculations: Birth details entered as an unauthenticated guest are processed in real-time in memory and are not permanently tied to a user identity.
  • Account Records: Birth profiles saved to your user account remain available until you choose to delete them.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You can edit or permanently delete any saved chart from your dashboard. If you wish to permanently delete your entire user account and associated records, email us at support@shriramjiastro.in. Your request will be fulfilled within 7 business days.
  • Tax & Accounting Records: Financial invoices and order transaction receipts are retained for the statutory period required by Indian tax laws.

8. International Users (अंतरराष्ट्रीय उपयोग)

Shri Ramji Astro operates globally and serves patrons across India, the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe, and Australia. If you access our services from outside India, please note that your information will be processed and stored on secure cloud servers in compliance with Indian data protection laws and international best practices. By using the platform, you acknowledge and consent to this transfer.

9. Children & Minors’ Privacy (बच्चों की गोपनीयता)

In classical Vedic tradition, Janam Kundlis and Namkaran Muhurtas are frequently created for newborns and children. Such astrological data must only be provided by the child's parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly solicit personal contact details directly from children under 18 years of age without parental consent.

10. Data Security Standards (डेटा सुरक्षा मानक)

We implement multi-layered administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • End-to-End Transport Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption protocols.
  • Database Hardening: Backend databases are secured with least-privilege role-based access control (RBAC) and row-level security policies.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Real-time server monitoring to detect and thwart anomalous traffic patterns, scrapers, or injection vulnerabilities.
