# Marketing Plan: Gulf Countries (GCC) — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain

November 2026. Target audience: Indian expatriate families living and working in the Gulf.
Goal: Direct Indian families in the GCC to buy wedding matching, wedding muhurta, and house-warming (Griha Pravesh) reports in US dollars.

---

## 1. Demographic and Cultural Reality

The Gulf region holds the largest concentration of Indian citizens outside India:
- **UAE:** ~3.8 million Indian residents (~38% of total UAE population).
- **Saudi Arabia:** ~2.5 million Indian residents.
- **Kuwait:** ~1.0 million Indian residents.
- **Qatar:** ~750,000 Indian residents.
- **Oman:** ~650,000 Indian residents.
- **Bahrain:** ~350,000 Indian residents.

**Total GCC Indian Diaspora:** Over 9 million people.

### Why English and Hindi (and NO Arabic marketing):
1. **Legal & Religious Restrictions:** Public advertising of astrology, fortune-telling, or occult services to Arabic-speaking Muslim citizens is prohibited or strictly regulated across GCC nations (UAE Federal Penal Code, Saudi Ministry of Commerce regulations).
2. **True Buyer Profile:** The buyers are Indian expatriate families (NRIs) who follow Vedic astrology (Jyotish). They speak Hindi, English, and regional Indian languages.
3. **The Gulf Expat Dilemma:**
   - Leave is strictly budgeted and scheduled months in advance.
   - Families arrange marriages or build homes back in India while working in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, or Doha.
   - They need exact muhurtas (wedding dates, foundation stone/Griha Pravesh dates) early so they can apply for workplace leave and book high-season international flights before prices spike.
