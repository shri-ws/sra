### A1. For questions like "Is Manglik dosha real? Should I worry?"

> Manglik (Kuja) dosha is real in the sense that the classical texts describe it, but most people who are told "you are Manglik" are given only half the rule.
>
> The basic rule: Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. Many apps check this only from the Ascendant. The traditional method checks it from three points, the Ascendant, the Moon and Venus, and then applies the exceptions (parihara) given in the texts: Mars in its own sign or exaltation, the aspect or conjunction of Jupiter, the sign of the house, and so on. In practice a large share of "Manglik" charts are cancelled once the exceptions are applied.
>
> Two practical points if you live outside India:
> 1. Your birth time must be converted with the daylight-saving rule that was in force at your birth place on that date. A one-hour error can move Mars into or out of the 7th house.
> 2. When two families compare, both charts should be checked the same way. A Manglik person matched with another Manglik person is a recognised cancellation.
>
> I run Shri Ramji Astro, where the Manglik report checks all three points, applies 21 classical exceptions and quotes the source verse for each. ### A2. For questions like "Is Gun Milan / kundli matching necessary for marriage?"

> Whether it is necessary is a family decision. What matters is reading the score correctly.
>
> Ashtakoota matching gives 36 points across 8 parts (Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot, Nadi). 18 is the usual minimum. But the total alone misleads both ways: a 28 can hide a Nadi dosha, and a 16 can have its Bhakoot dosha cancelled by the rules in Muhurta Chintamani and related texts (for example when the Moon signs share a lord or are friends). Matching also says nothing about Manglik dosha, which is a separate check on the full chart.
>
> So if your family asks for matching, ask for: the score for each koota, whether Nadi and Bhakoot dosha are present and cancelled, and both Manglik checks. Our Gun Milan + Manglik report does exactly that, with the verse quoted for each rule, for USD 21: https://shriramjiastro.in/en/kundli-matching.html?utm_source=quora

### A3. For questions like "What is the difference between Vedic and Western astrology?"

> Three differences matter in practice:
> 1. **Zodiac.** Vedic (Jyotish) uses the sidereal zodiac, tied to the stars; Western uses the tropical zodiac, tied to the seasons. Today they differ by about 24 degrees, so most people's Sun sign moves back one sign.
> 2. **Nakshatras.** Vedic astrology divides the sky into 27 lunar mansions. Your Moon's nakshatra drives much of the reading.
> 3. **Dashas.** Vedic astrology times events with planetary periods (Vimshottari dasha: 120 years split among nine planets), not only with transits.
>
> The main classical source is Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS), 97 chapters in most editions. If you want to see your own chart read chapter by chapter from that text, our Mega Parashara report does that for USD 21, calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris and the Lahiri ayanamsa: https://shriramjiastro.in/en/vedic-birth-chart-report.html?utm_source=quora
