Why it helps

  • Leave from a Gulf job is fixed months ahead, and flights home get dear in the wedding season. A date found early is a date you can plan around.
  • The panchang's general muhurta is the same for everyone. Here the dates come from the couple's own charts.
  • Each date shows why it is pure or not: the 10 classical mahadoshas (Latta, Pata, Jamitra, Vedha, Bana, Ekargala, Upagraha, Dagdha tithi, Krantisamya, Shanku) are screened one by one.

What you get

  • Pure wedding dates and lagna times for the bride and groom
  • Tithi, vara, nakshatra, yoga and lagna shuddhi checked for each date
  • The reason a date is rejected, in plain words, with the shloka
  • Times for the place where the wedding will be held

Good to know: Enter the town in India (or wherever the wedding will be) as the place; the times are for that place.
