Why it helps

  • Many families in the Gulf build a house in India while they work abroad. The owner usually has to be there for the foundation and the house-warming, so the date has to fit the leave.
  • A muhurta found from the owner's own chart, for the town where the house stands, is not the same as a general date from a calendar.
  • Every date carries the reason and the shloka, so the family pandit at home can check it.

What you get

  • Neev pujan (foundation) and Griha pravesh dates and times
  • Checks of tithi, vara, nakshatra, lagna and the owner's chart
  • Times for the town where the house is
  • A4 PDF to print or send to the family

Good to know: Give the town where the house is as the place; the times are for that town.
