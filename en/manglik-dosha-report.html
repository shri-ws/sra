Many apps call a chart Manglik when Mars sits in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from the Ascendant, and stop there. The classical method checks from three points and then applies the exceptions. Often the dosha turns out to be cancelled.

How the check works

1. Three reference points

Mars is checked from the Ascendant, from the Moon and from Venus, the three lagnas used for marriage.

2. Navamsha confirmation

The position of Mars in the D9 chart, and whether it is vargottama, refines how strong the dosha is.

3. 21 classical cancellations

Mars in its own or exaltation sign, the aspect or company of Jupiter, the sign of the house and the other exceptions in the texts are applied one by one.

4. A net result

The report gives the dosha that remains after cancellations, as a percentage, and what it means for matching.

Planning a marriage?

A Manglik dosha is judged against the partner's chart: when both partners carry it, it is classically treated as balanced. If you already have both charts, the kundli matching report (USD 21) checks Manglik for both partners together with the 36-point Gun Milan.

Born outside India?

One hour can move Mars into or out of the 7th house. Enter the birth city; the page applies the time zone and the daylight-saving rule that was in force there on the birth date.
