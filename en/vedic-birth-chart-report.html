New to Vedic astrology?

Three things differ from the astrology in Western magazines:

  • The zodiac is sidereal. It is tied to the stars rather than the seasons. Today the two zodiacs differ by about 24 degrees, so your Sun sign here is often the sign before your Western one.
  • The Moon's nakshatra matters most. The sky is divided into 27 lunar mansions; the one your Moon sits in shapes much of the reading.
  • Timing comes from dashas. Your life is divided into planetary periods (Vimshottari dasha, 120 years in all), and the report dates each one for you.

How it is calculated

  • Swiss Ephemeris (JPL DE431), Lahiri (True Chitra Paksha) ayanamsa with nutation.
  • Sunrise from the centre of the Sun's disc with refraction, which fixes the special lagnas exactly.
  • Whole-sign houses, which stay stable even for births at high latitudes (Scandinavia, northern Canada).
  • Births outside India: the page applies the time zone and daylight-saving rule of the birth place on the birth date.
